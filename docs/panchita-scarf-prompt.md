# Panchita’s autumn logo

The user requested scarf-wearing Panchita as the village logo and explicitly kept the original garden landscape. The standalone logo above the garden title and the footer use `public/holiday/panchita-scarf.png`. The garden, postcard and village invitation still use the unchanged `public/holiday/gratitude-garden.png`.

Panchita’s identity reference was the existing Codex pet sprite sheet, also present at `sites/travel/dist/assets/panchita.png`: cream fleece, rosy cheeks, dark eyes, and a pink bow. No Codex desktop pet was created or changed.

The logo was created using the built-in imagegen tool with a transparent background. Final prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Reference image: the provided sprite sheet shows the established identity of Panchita, the user's cream-colored alpaca mascot. Create ONE large standalone full-body illustration of Panchita, not a sheet or multiple poses. Translate her recognizable cream fluffy fleece, round little face, large glossy dark eyes, tiny smiling mouth, rosy pink cheeks, small brown hooves, and pink bow beside one ear into a delicate hand-painted storybook watercolor and colored-pencil style. Add a cozy warm burnt-orange knitted scarf around her neck with two short hanging fringed ends. Retain the pink bow; she must unmistakably be this character. Friendly slightly three-quarter view, head facing viewer, body gently angled, standing on all four little legs. A modest natural alpaca neck, round cuddly body, charming slightly oversized head and perked ears. Relaxed happy welcoming expression. No other characters, no leaves, no props, no scenery, no ground shadow. Soft autumn palette: ivory cream, blush pink, terracotta scarf. Fine warm brown pencil details, textured watercolor fleece, subtle natural irregular edges. All ears, bow, tail and hooves comfortably inside the frame, subject centered with a little clear margin. Actual transparent background, no paper rectangle, no checkerboard, no text, no border. This is a garden website mascot and should remain readable at 100px tall.

The leaf picker retains its five existing values and radio-button behavior. The illustrations now have individual silhouettes and veins, subtle color washes and grain, larger previews, hover movement, and a selected outline/check mark. They remain inline SVGs with unique React IDs for their gradients and clipping paths.
